About Us

Bridging Theory & Practice

The Institute of Digital Risk (IDR) is a forward-thinking training and deployment institute focused on the rapidly evolving fields of digital, cyber and technology risk. Our mission is to equip the next generation of professionals with the expertise required to operate in high-consequence, regulated environments.

Working in collaboration with leading academic partners and industry experts, IDR connects theoretical research with practical implementation. Our programs focus on real-world problem solving, preparing practitioners who can design, implement and manage modern risk frameworks.

Our Pillars

Service Pillars

Academy

Professional training programs and bootcamps designed for students and early-career professionals. The academy provides hands-on learning in cyber risk, digital governance, AI oversight and technology assurance.

Innovation & Incubation

A research and innovation hub focused on developing future digital risk models, AI governance frameworks and new intellectual property that helps organisations manage emerging technology risks.

Advisory Services

Strategic advisory services helping organisations implement and strengthen risk management frameworks such as NIST, ISO 27001 and NIS2. IDR supports companies in building resilient digital infrastructures.
